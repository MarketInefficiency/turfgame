Privacy Policy

Turfgame.io · Last updated June 16, 2026

Turfgame.io ("Turfgame", "we", "us") is a free multiplayer browser and mobile game. We built it to be played instantly with as little personal information as possible. You can play the whole game as a guest. This policy explains what we handle, what we store if you make an account, and what we don't do.

Playing as a guest

You can play without an account, and as a guest we do not collect personal information about you.

  • Display name. The name you type before playing is stored on your own device (in local storage) so it can be filled in next time. During a match it is sent to the game server so other players can see it on your avatar. It is not kept after your session ends.
  • Gameplay data. While you play, the server processes your inputs (movement and claims) to run the match. This is transient and is not tied to your identity.
  • No ads, no tracking. We do not show ads, we do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not build a profile of you or sell your data.

If you create an account

Making an account is optional. You only need one if you want to keep your name, medals, and cosmetics across devices. When you create one, we store:

  • your email address, so we can sign you in and help you recover the account;
  • your username;
  • your medal balance and the cosmetics you own and have equipped;
  • a record of any purchases or membership so they stay attached to your account.

This is stored in our backend, which runs on Supabase (it handles sign-in and our database). We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Signing in with Google or Apple

You can sign in with an email and password, or with your Google or Apple account. If you use Google or Apple, they confirm who you are and share your email address so we can create or find your account. We never receive your Google or Apple password. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, Apple gives us a private relay address instead of your real one, and the game works the same either way. Google and Apple handle the sign-in under their own privacy policies.

Purchases

Medals, the membership, and Remove Ads can be bought in the game. On the website, payments go through Stripe. In the mobile apps, payments go through the Apple App Store (and Google Play on Android), with RevenueCat helping us match a purchase to your account. We keep a record that a purchase happened so we can grant what you bought, but we never see or store your full card details. Those stay with the payment processor or app store, under their own policies.

Third-party platforms and services

You might play Turfgame on a game portal such as CrazyGames, or through the Apple App Store or Google Play, and those platforms have their own privacy policies for anything they collect. To run the game we also rely on a few services: Supabase for accounts and data, Stripe for web payments, and the app stores plus RevenueCat for mobile purchases. Each handles data under its own policy, and we encourage you to review the ones that apply to you.

Children's privacy

Turfgame is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Data retention and your choices

Your display name lives in your device's local storage, and you can clear it any time in your browser or device settings. Transient gameplay data is not kept after a match. If you make an account, you can delete it at any time from inside the game: open the account panel and choose Delete my account. That removes your profile, your medal balance, and your cosmetics record from our database. You can also email us if you would rather we handle it for you.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game grows. If we ever add something new, such as ads, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above before it takes effect.
