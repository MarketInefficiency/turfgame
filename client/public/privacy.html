Privacy Policy

Turfgame.io · Last updated June 10, 2026

Turfgame.io ("Turfgame", "we", "us") is a free multiplayer browser and mobile game. We built it to be played instantly with as little personal information as possible. This policy explains what we handle and what we don't.

Information we collect

Right now, the game requires no account and we do not collect personal information. Specifically:

  • Display name. The name you type before playing is stored locally on your own device (in your browser's local storage) so it can be filled in next time. During a match it is sent to the game server so other players can see it on your avatar. It is not stored after your session ends.
  • Gameplay data. While you play, the server processes your inputs (movement and claims) to run the match. This is transient and is not tied to your identity.
  • No tracking. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not build a profile of you.

Accounts and purchases (planned)

In the future the game will offer an optional account so you can keep your progress and cosmetics. If you choose to create one, we will store only what is needed to provide it:

  • your account username;
  • a record of cosmetic purchases you make — purchased skins, capital designs, and sword designs — so they stay unlocked on your account.

We will not sell your information or use it for advertising. Payments, if offered, will be handled by the app store or a payment processor under their own privacy policies; we do not store your full payment details.

Third-party platforms

You may play Turfgame on third-party game portals (for example, CrazyGames) or through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Those platforms have their own privacy policies covering any data they collect, including any ads they may show. We encourage you to review the policy of whichever platform you use.

Children's privacy

Turfgame is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Data retention & your choices

Your display name lives only in your device's local storage; you can clear it any time through your browser settings. Transient gameplay data is not retained after a match. If accounts are introduced, you will be able to request deletion of your account and its data by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game grows (for example, when accounts or purchases launch). We will revise the "Last updated" date above when we do.

Questions about this policy? Email support@turfgame.io.
